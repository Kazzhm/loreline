## Official Tracks

1. **Audience growth & engagement** — help creators find, grow, and retain an audience.
2. **Content repurposing across platforms** — adapt content for different channels and formats.
3. **Moderation & community assistance** — context-aware moderation and community nurturing.

Loreline targets **Moderation & community assistance**.

## Required Technology and Minds Requirements

Every entry must include a persistent Mind that solves a genuine creator-economy problem. The Mind must be integral to the final product rather than optional or peripheral. Official guidance calls for:

- memory across sessions;
- continuity that resumes prior work;
- autonomous follow-up without constant human prompting.

The requirements section also states that the persistence demonstration may show memory, continuity, or autonomous follow-up. Because the detail page presents all three as expected behavior, Loreline treats all three as submission targets.

Other services may be used, but the Mind must remain central to product operation.

## Submission Requirements

- Working product with a Mind integral to core operations.
- Persistence demonstration across sessions.
- Clear solution to one official creator-economy Track.
- Demo video lasting **1.5–2 minutes** explaining the product and showing it working.
- Public code repository link through GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket.
- Technical documentation.

The exact DoraHacks form fields beyond these requirements are `UNVERIFIED` until a submission draft is opened.

## Judging Criteria

Each criterion is scored from 1 to 10:

1. Minds Integration Depth
2. Creator-Economy Problem Fit
3. Innovation & Creativity
4. Execution & Completeness
5. Viability & Scalability

Judges select the winner in a tie.
