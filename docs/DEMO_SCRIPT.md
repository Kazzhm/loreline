# Demo Script — Target 1:50

## 0:00–0:12 — Problem

"A growing creator community produces more lore, ideas, and remixes than one person can remember. The hard part is not deleting bad comments. It is deciding what can safely become part of the world—and preserving who contributed it."

## 0:12–0:23 — Product

"Loreline is a persistent canon and community steward. It remembers the creator's rules, prior exceptions, contributor history, and unresolved cases."

## 0:23–0:43 — Session A

Open Session A. Store: "The Glass Sea cannot be crossed at night; moonlight turns every vessel back toward its port of origin."

Show the real Mind reply. Leave Session A and switch to Session B.

## 0:43–1:06 — Session B and Magic Moment

Open a separate session. Submit only: "A fan proposes a courier who crosses the Glass Sea at midnight by sailing without a lantern."

Point out that the prompt does not repeat the canon rule. Show:

- Context loaded
- Precedent recalled
- Decision prepared

Read one short line from the real Mind response explaining the conflict.

## 1:06–1:22 — Session C and Continuity

Show the case remains open with a requested revision. Submit only the daylight revision in Session C. Show that the Mind recalls the rule and prior issue, then accepts the change. If verified autonomy evidence is available, reveal it briefly; otherwise do not claim it.

## 1:22–1:35 — Attribution

Approve the revision. Show the contribution receipt with content digest, creator approval, contributor address if present, and test-network verification.

## 1:35–1:45 — Architecture

"Minds holds the creator relationship and reasoning continuity. Loreline stores workflow state and approval gates. The chain stores only portable receipt evidence."

## 1:45–1:50 — Close

"Loreline lets creator communities grow without losing the world, the precedent, or the people who helped build it."

## Recording Rules

- Record only real API responses.
- Do not show secrets, console credentials, or private keys.
- Keep the full video between 1.5 and 2 minutes.
- Show the session boundary visibly.
- Do not spend time on the landing section beyond product orientation.
