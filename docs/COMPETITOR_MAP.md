## Crowding Assessment

- **Generic creator operating system:** already represented strongly by Muse; avoid.
- **Generic moderation memory:** represented by Keeper; a new entry needs a narrower high-value object than message moderation.
- **Repurposing plus provenance:** represented by ProvenaMind; avoid a clip-and-hash pipeline.

## White Space

The clearest public gap is the boundary between community management and creator IP: remembering a fictional world's canon, reviewing community contributions against creator-specific precedent, preserving consent, and creating portable attribution when a contribution is accepted. Loreline occupies this gap.

## Competitive Design Rules for Loreline

1. Do not become another general moderation queue.
2. Make canon memory and creator precedent visible in the demo.
3. Treat approved community contributions as rights-bearing work, not merely safe comments.
4. Keep the chain role limited to portable receipt and agreement evidence.
5. Demonstrate one emotionally legible submission from conflict to approved co-creation.
